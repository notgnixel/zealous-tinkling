What is your hair porosity? *

Hair porosity refers to your hair's ability to absorb/retain moisture.

To find out: drop a couple strands into a cup of water and wait 2–4 minutes. If your hair floats → low porosity. If it sinks → high porosity. If it stays in the middle → medium porosity.

What is your hair density? *

Hair density refers to the amount of hair on your head.

Part a front section with your hands: if you can clearly see your scalp → thin. If you can barely see it → thick. Somewhere in between → medium.
