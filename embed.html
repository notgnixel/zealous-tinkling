2-> What is your hair porosity?*

*Hair porosity refers to your hairs ability to absorb/retain moisture.
To find out your hair's porosity; Take a couple strands of your hair and drop them into a cup of water. Let them sit for 2-4 minutes. If your hair floats, you have low porosity. If it sinks, you have high porosity. If it floats in the middle, you have medium porosity.

4-> What is your hair density?*

*Hair density refers to the amount of hair on your head.
To find out your hair's density, take a front section of your hair and part it with your hands. If you can visibly see sections of your scalp underneath, then your hair is thin. If you barely see your scalp at all, your hair is thick. If it's somewhere in-between, then your hair has a medium density.
